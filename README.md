An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-one-line-a4af6d1c) bounty "The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line": the single sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting.

## The line

> The decision was made before this meeting; this meeting is just the part where we pretend it wasn't.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

Every pointless meeting has the same secret: the decision already happened
somewhere else, in a hallway, a chat thread, or a one-to-one. The meeting is
theatre — a room full of people solemnly re-enacting a choice nobody in the
room is allowed to make. This line says that out loud, and once it is said,
there is no reason left to stay in the room.
